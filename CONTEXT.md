# Personal money tracking

A private record of the owner’s incoming and outgoing card transactions.

## Language

**Transaction**: One incoming or outgoing card movement reported by UZCARD. Language copies in one notification describe the same transaction.

**Spending**: The sum of outgoing transactions, including payments and outgoing card-to-card transfers.

**Income**: Incoming money, including salary, reimbursements, and other incoming transfers.

**Salary**: Income paid by an employer. Separate instalments are separate transactions, even within the same month.

**Reimbursement**: Incoming money repaying someone else’s share of a bill the owner paid. It is income in the combined view, not additional spending.

**Net cash flow**: Income minus spending for the selected transactions in one currency. It is not a bank account balance.

**Review item**: A notification whose financial fields or operation cannot yet be interpreted reliably. It is excluded from all totals until resolved.
